Spot-first, without the pain

Moved workloads to spot instances. Cluster Autoscaler node groups plus graceful pod termination and replicas meant interruptions became background noise.

eks · aks · cluster-autoscaler

Hub-and-spoke → one cluster

Started with Rancher managing several clusters, then consolidated dev and staging into one. Fewer moving parts, same isolation through namespaces.

rancher · eksctl → terraform

300 cores for a solver

Load-tested a CPU-bound battery-optimization solver with Locust on AKS + Knative, then moved it where cost/performance was best.

knative · locust · lambda
